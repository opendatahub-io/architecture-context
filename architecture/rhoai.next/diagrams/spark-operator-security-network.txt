FIPS Compliance Notes:
- spark-operator binary: FIPS-compliant (GOEXPERIMENT=strictfipsruntime, CGO_ENABLED=1)
- spark-operator-module-controller: NOT FIPS-compliant (CGO_ENABLED=0, no FIPS flags)
  → Potential check-payload compliance gap
- crypto/md5 used in pkg/util/sparkapplication.go for deterministic pod naming
  (non-cryptographic use, not a FIPS concern)
