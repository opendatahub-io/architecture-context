Security Risks:
===============
- ⚠ payload-processing DestinationRule uses insecureSkipVerify=true for TLS origination
  (mesh-internal, relies on Istio mTLS for identity verification)
- ⚠ Internal maas-api endpoints (8080/TCP) use plaintext HTTP
  (protected by NetworkPolicy, not encryption)
