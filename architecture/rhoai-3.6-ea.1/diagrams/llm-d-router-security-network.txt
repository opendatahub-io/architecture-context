RBAC Summary:
- No ClusterRoles or RoleBindings are shipped in this repository.
- RBAC is provisioned by the deploying operator/controller at deployment time.
- The EPP requires watch/list/get permissions on:
  - core/v1 Pods (PodReconciler)
  - llm-d.ai/v1alpha1 EndpointPickerConfig
  - llm-d.ai/v1alpha2 InferenceModelRewrite
  - llm-d.ai/v1alpha2 InferenceObjective
  - inference.networking.k8s.io/v1 InferencePool

Authentication & Authorization:
- ExtProc gRPC (9002): No application-level auth; TLS transport only (SecureServing=true)
- Health gRPC (9003): No application-level auth; TLS transport only (SecureServing=true)
- Kubernetes API: kubeconfig credential chain via controller-runtime
- Authentication delegated to platform network policies and service mesh
