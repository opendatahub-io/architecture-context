  1. spark-operator-controller
     - ClusterRole: spark-operator-controller
     - Resources: pods (CRUD), services (CRUD), configmaps (CRUD), events,
       ingresses (CRUD), poddisruptionbudgets (CRUD),
       sparkapplications/scheduledsparkapplications/sparkconnects (watch/status)
     - Notable: apiextensions.k8s.io/customresourcedefinitions (get),
       config.openshift.io/apiservers (get)
     - Leader election: coordination.k8s.io/leases (create, get, update)

  2. spark-operator-webhook
     - ClusterRole: spark-operator-webhook
     - Resources: pods (list, watch), resourcequotas (list),
       webhook configurations (get, list, watch, update),
       sparkapplications/scheduledsparkapplications (get, list, watch)
     - Secrets: RESTRICTED to resourceNames ["spark-operator-webhook-certs"]
     - Leader election: coordination.k8s.io/leases (create, get, update)

  3. spark-operator-module-controller-manager
     - ClusterRole: spark-operator-module-manager-role
     - Resources: BROAD - deployments, CRDs, RBAC (with escalate/bind),
       webhook configs, cert-manager CRs, networkpolicies, podmonitors,
       configmaps, services, serviceaccounts, namespaces
     - SparkOperator CR: full lifecycle (list, watch, get, patch, update)
     - Leader election: coordination.k8s.io/leases (full CRUD)

User-facing ClusterRoles:
  - spark-operator-sparkapplication-viewer-role: get, list, watch
  - spark-operator-sparkapplication-editor-role: full CRUD
  - spark-operator-scheduledsparkapplication-viewer-role: get, list, watch
  - spark-operator-scheduledsparkapplication-editor-role: full CRUD

Webhook Security:
  - All 7 webhooks use failurePolicy: Fail
  - Webhook unavailability BLOCKS Spark workload creation/updates
  - TLS certificates: self-managed CA or cert-manager provisioned
  - Secret access restricted by resourceNames (least privilege)

FIPS Compliance:
  - Build: GOEXPERIMENT=strictfipsruntime, CGO_ENABLED=1
  - Runtime: Panics if FIPS mode cannot be initialized (fail-closed)
  - Crypto: stdlib crypto/tls routed through system OpenSSL
  - Cipher suites: Only CipherSuites() (no InsecureCipherSuites())
