FIPS Compliance Notes:
  - TLS: rustls 0.23 with ring backend (NOT FIPS 140-2/3 validated)
  - Crypto: ring 0.17 (AES-256-GCM, SHA-256) - NIST algorithms but no FIPS certification
  - No OpenSSL present - check-payload FIPS gate may not pass
  - aws_lc_rs (jsonwebtoken) has FIPS module but activation not configured

Security Boundaries:
  - UID separation: Sidecar (UID 1337) / Workload (UID 1000+)
  - Credential isolation: TLS material at 0o750/0o400 permissions
  - nftables bypass: Sidecar traffic not re-proxied
  - IPC: Unix domain sockets with peer credential validation
  - L4+L7 defense-in-depth: nftables filtering → OPA policy → L7 inspection
  - Formal verification: Z3 SMT solver for policy exfiltration path detection
