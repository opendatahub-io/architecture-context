Cryptographic Architecture:
- TLS Provider: rustls 0.23.45 (protocol) + rustls-openssl 0.4.1 (crypto)
- Crypto Backend: System OpenSSL libcrypto.so (never vendored)
- Provider Install: Once at startup, before any listener/connector
- Custom Provider: Pingora fork's "custom-provider" removes rustls fallback
- FIPS Build: Excludes policy-engine feature (sha2/hmac/jsonwebtoken)
- FIPS Enforcement: PRAXIS_REQUIRE_FIPS=1 checks kernel flag + OpenSSL status
- Allocator: jemalloc on Unix (reduced contention)

  It does not create or require RBAC resources.
