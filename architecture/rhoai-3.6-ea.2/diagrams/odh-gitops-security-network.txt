Authentication & Authorization:
- KServe inference Gateway: Authorino via RHCL/Kuadrant, Istio sidecar enforcement
- MaaS Gateway: Authorino via RHCL/Kuadrant, Istio sidecar enforcement
- Operator metrics: None (ClusterIP internal access only)

TLS Certificate Management:
- Gateway certificates: cert-manager with configurable ClusterIssuer/Issuer (default: rhai-ca-issuer)
- CA bundle: Distributed as ConfigMaps across namespaces for internal trust
- Authorino CA trust: Injected via SSL_CERT_FILE environment variable
- All crypto delegated to cert-manager and Istio (no direct crypto operations)
