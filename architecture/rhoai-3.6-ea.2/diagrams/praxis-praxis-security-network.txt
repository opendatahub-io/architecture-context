- Check: /proc/sys/crypto/fips_enabled + provider FIPS status
- Enforcement: PRAXIS_REQUIRE_FIPS env var refuses startup if not FIPS
- Exclusions: Policy engine (ppe, sha2, hmac, aws-lc-rs) excluded from FIPS build
- Cargo.lock: --locked enforced in both Containerfiles
