    Auto-Rotate: No

    Purpose: TLS serving certificate for /metrics endpoint
    Provisioned by: OpenShift service-ca (via annotation)
    Auto-Rotate: Yes

TLS Configuration:
  - MinVersion: TLS 1.2 (tls.VersionTLS12)
  - ALPN: h2, http/1.1
  - Certificate sources: custom CA bundle (PROMETHEUS_CA_CERT_PATH),
    client certs, or system trust store
  - InsecureSkipVerify: configurable, defaults to false on OpenShift

FIPS Compliance:
  - Build: GOEXPERIMENT=strictfipsruntime, CGO_ENABLED=1
  - Linking: Dynamic (links to OpenSSL via UBI9)
  - Crypto: stdlib crypto/tls (FIPS via OpenSSL with strictfipsruntime)
  - Risks: None detected (no MD5/RC4/DES usage)

Architectural Risk Note:
  The controller requires cluster-wide read access to nodes, pods, namespaces,
  and services for GPU inventory discovery and pod-to-variant attribution.
  A compromised controller pod could enumerate significant cluster state.
