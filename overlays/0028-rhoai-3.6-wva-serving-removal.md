## Fact

For `rhoai.next`, Workload Variant Autoscaler (WVA) autoscaling is removed from
the LLM-D/KServe serving path.

## Impact on Strategies

- Do not assume WVA autoscaling is available for `rhoai.next` LLM-D or KServe workloads.
- Treat `llm-d-workload-variant-autoscaler` and `workload-variant-autoscaler` as aliases for the same WVA lineage; exclude both from the `rhoai.next` component inventory.
- Confirm the supported replacement for replica management before designing autoscaling for `rhoai.next`.

## Context

The two names represent the upstream LLM-D repository and its downstream
RHOAI repository. The current `rhoai.next` inventory contains both aliases;
the next generation must remove both.
