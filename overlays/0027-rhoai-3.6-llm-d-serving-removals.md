## Fact

Starting with RHOAI 3.6-ea.2, the LLM-D/KServe serving path no longer includes
`llm-d-latency-predictor`. This removal continues through RHOAI 3.6 GA and
later rolling releases.

## Impact on Strategies

- Do not model `llm-d-latency-predictor` as an active LLM-D or KServe dependency for RHOAI 3.6 or later.
- Confirm the supported latency-routing behavior before designing a later RHOAI serving path.

## Context

The generated RHOAI 3.6-ea.2 architecture inventory was created before this
serving-path change and still lists the removed repository. The 3.6-ea.2 and
rolling `rhoai.next` platform definitions exclude its discovered component
alias on the next generation run; this overlay corrects consumers until that
regeneration occurs.
